This repository consolidates the full suite of bioinformatic analysis pipelines established for the Independent Research Project (BS7130), with the primary objective of elucidating conserved fibrotic protein signatures across idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) and aortic stenosis (AS) through DIA-MS proteomics. Three discrete differential expression analysis pipelines were systematically constructed in R (v4.5.1), encompassing all stages from data preprocessing and differential expression analysis to data visualisation and network-based drug repurposing. The methodological framework was adapted and refined from Maxwell et al. (2025), European Journal of Pharmacology, to ensure methodological rigour and reproducibility.

A consistent preprocessing framework was applied across all pipelines, incorporating 70% completeness filtering, log₂ median centring normalisation, NOISeq abundance and MAD-based feature filtering, near-zero variance removal via the Caret package, and ARSyNseq donor batch effect correction. Differential expression analysis was conducted using limma (v3.66.0) with empirical Bayes moderation (eBayes; trend=TRUE, robust=TRUE) and Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction. To address the constraints of pilot-scale sample sizes (n=3–5 donors), a tiered significance threshold of FDR<0.1 and |log₂FC|>0.58 was uniformly applied. For the AS drug contrast pipeline, robust=TRUE was additionally specified to mitigate variance inflation associated with single technical replicate measurement. Full reproducibility was ensured through the implementation of a fixed random seed (set.seed(42)). The analytical workflow was supported by a suite of R packages, including limma (v3.66.0), NOISeq, Caret, ggplot2 (v4.0.3), pheatmap (v1.0.12), clusterProfiler (v4.18.4), ggVennDiagram (v1.5.7), igraph, ggraph, and tidygraph.

Raw proteomic data are derived from human IPF lung tissue, AS aortic valve tissue, and healthy lung explant donors and are subject to ethical restrictions. Data are not publicly available. All analysis code is fully reproducible (once the directory is corrected & personalised) given the preprocessed expression matrices.

AI assistance (Claude, Anthropic, 2026) was used in the planning and understanding of certain complex code aspects, as well as in the resolution of errors.
